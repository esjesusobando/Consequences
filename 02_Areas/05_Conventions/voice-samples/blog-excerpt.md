## Why Most Tutorials Fail

Tutorials teach you to build a todo app. Then you open a real codebase and realize you know nothing.

The gap isn't technical — it's contextual. Tutorials strip away the mess: legacy code, unclear requirements, team politics, production constraints. Real engineering happens in that mess.

What if tutorials embraced the mess instead of hiding it?

Start with a broken codebase. Introduce one problem at a time. Let the learner experience the decision-making process, not just the final solution.
