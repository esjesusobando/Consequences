> **Instrucciones:** Reemplaza con un post de LinkedIn que hayas publicado y te guste.

## Ejemplo

Most engineering teams don't have a code review problem. They have a feedback problem.

Reviews happen. Comments get left. But the feedback is vague, inconsistent, and often ignored.

The fix isn't more reviews — it's better feedback structure.

3 things that changed our review quality:
1. Every comment must include "why" — not just "change this"
2. Blockers vs suggestions — explicit labels, no ambiguity
3. One round of review, then merge — no endless back-and-forth

Result: review cycle dropped from 3 days to 18 hours.
