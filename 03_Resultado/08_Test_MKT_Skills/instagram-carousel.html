Sign #1: Scope creep is your default

The problem: Briefs arrive vague. Feedback is endless. "One more revision" becomes 7 more revisions.

The fix: Standardize your intake. One brief template. One feedback round built into scope. Changes = new SOW.

Teams using structured briefs reduce revision cycles by 60%.

Sign #2: "Which version is final?"

The problem: Files everywhere. Google Drive, Dropbox, email attachments. Nobody knows what's current.

The fix: Single source of truth. One tool. One folder structure. One feedback loop. Everything else is draft.

Creative teams waste 12 hrs/week searching for files.

Sign #3: Meetings > Making

The problem: "Quick sync" becomes 3 hours of updates. No one has uninterrupted creation time.

The fix: Time-block creation. 4-hour deep work blocks. No Slack. No meetings. Protected by default.
