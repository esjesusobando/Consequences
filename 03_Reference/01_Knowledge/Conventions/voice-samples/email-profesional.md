# Email Profesional — Template

> **Instrucciones:** Reemplaza este contenido con un email real que hayas enviado a un VP, cliente o colega formal. Mantén el formato original.

## Ejemplo

**Para:** vp@empresa.com
**Asunto:** Update on Q3 roadmap

---

Hi [Name],

Quick update on the Q3 roadmap. We're on track for the July launch, but there are two items that need your input:

1. **Pricing tier restructuring** — engineering completed the backend changes, but we need sign-off on the new tiers before we can proceed.

2. **Enterprise feature flag** — the team flagged a dependency on the auth migration. If we don't resolve this by Friday, it pushes the timeline by 2 weeks.

Can we sync for 15 minutes tomorrow to decide on these?

Best,
[Your name]
