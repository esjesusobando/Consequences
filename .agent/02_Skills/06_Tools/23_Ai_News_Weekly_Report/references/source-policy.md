# Source policy for AI weekly news briefings

Use this reference when improving or manually verifying the generated report.

## Source priority

1. Primary sources: company blog posts, research papers, model cards, official product docs, regulator/government releases.
2. Reputable secondary sources: Reuters, AP, Financial Times, The Verge, TechCrunch, Wired, Bloomberg, CNBC, The Information, Ars Technica, MIT Technology Review.
3. Aggregators/RSS/search results: acceptable for discovery, but verify important claims against a primary or reputable source when possible.

## Citation rules

- Keep a source URL per major item.
- Distinguish confirmed facts from interpretation.
- Do not present rumors as facts.
- For launches/model releases, include the company/lab, product/model name, date, and direct source URL when available.
- For policy/regulation, include jurisdiction, agency/body, effective date if stated, and source URL.

## Editorial angles

For each selected item, answer:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter this week?
- Who is affected?
- What should builders/operators/watchers do next?
- What remains uncertain?
