# Paul Iusztin

## Background

Paul Iusztin (also referred to as Paul Yushin in the transcript) is the founder and CEO of [Decoding AI](https://decodingai.com), where he produces content and courses on shipping AI products. He is the co-author of the *LLM Engineers Handbook* bestseller. He brings 18 months of personal experience building a second brain with over 5,000 notes in Obsidian and 5,000 in Readwise.

## Role in AI Research OS

Paul is the primary architect of the AI Research OS. He designed the three-version evolution (V1 → V2 → V3) based on his own workflow needs, and he authored the deep research algorithm that forms the core of the system. His perspective is shaped by being a content creator who needs to manage vast amounts of research material — articles, courses, code, and videos — and efficiently retrieve relevant notes when starting new projects.

## Key Contributions

- **V1 design**: Created the initial deep research algorithm targeting the public web with handpicked golden links, producing static research MD files for course lessons (35 lessons generated quickly).
- **V2 pivot**: Identified the limitation of targeting only the public web and redirected the deep research loop to target the user's own second brain sources (Obsidian, Readwise, Notebook LM, GitHub).
- **V3 architecture**: Designed the three-layer architecture (raw, index, wiki) that transformed the system from producing static files to generating a living, queryable wiki.
- **Workflow philosophy**: Advocated for plain Markdown files over vector databases, emphasizing that "the project is to teach AI engineering" and keep systems inspectable.
- **PARA method integration**: Applied Tiago Forte's PARA organizational framework to structure the Obsidian vault and the wiki layer.

## Approach and Philosophy

Paul emphasizes that the system should be personal — anchored into personal notes, values, and faith. He wants the system to reflect his own thoughts, not just aggregate generic information. His approach is builder-oriented: the system is designed to be tweaked, extended, and adapted to individual needs rather than shipped as a polished product.
