# Louis-François Bouchard

## Background

Louis-François Bouchard is the co-founder and CTO of [Towards AI](https://towards.ai), where the team builds educational courses on AI engineering. He is the creator of *What's AI*, a YouTube channel explaining AI engineering techniques. He is also the author of *Building AI Systems for Production*. Before entering industry, he completed a PhD in AI and conducted extensive academic research. He describes himself as someone who "makes research for a living" — from PhD work to building courses, writing videos, and training companies.

## Role in AI Research OS

Louis-François is the co-architect and systems thinker behind the AI Research OS. While Paul focused on the deep research algorithm and workflow design, Louis contributed the architectural vision: the three-layer system, the reference-based indexing approach, and the emphasis on context engineering as the core discipline. He demonstrated the system through three practical examples (article research, GitHub repository ingestion, and simple link ingestion).

## Key Contributions

- **Architectural design**: Defined the three-layer architecture (raw/, index.yaml, wiki/) and the principle of avoiding vector databases and knowledge graphs in favor of plain files.
- **Context engineering framing**: Articulated the relationship between filesystem (long-term memory), RAM (short-term memory), and the context window as the engineering constraint that must be managed.
- **V2/V3 evolution**: Drove the shift from public-web-only research (V1) to second-brain targeting (V2) and the living wiki layer (V3), recognizing that static research files don't scale.
- **Demo execution**: Ran the three demonstration examples showing the system in action — deep research on an article, GitHub repository ingestion, and simple link ingestion — proving the system works across complexity levels.
- **Philosophy of teaching**: Consistently emphasized that the project's goal is to teach AI engineering principles (memory management, context engineering) rather than build a polished product.

## Approach and Philosophy

Louis advocates for simplicity and education over product polish. He deliberately chose a "builder workflow" — using Codex and Claude Code in the terminal — because it aligns with the teaching objective. He acknowledges weaknesses (hard to detect outdated sources, limited connectors) but considers them out of scope for the core educational mission.

> "The thing is that we actually build all of that into another product that you can even build yourself." — referring to the Agent Engineering course.
