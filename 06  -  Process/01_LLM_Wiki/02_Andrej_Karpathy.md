# Andrej Karpathy

## Resumen
AI researcher and former Director of AI at Tesla. Creator of the LLM Wiki pattern for Obsidian. Advocates for persistent knowledge bases using LLMs instead of traditional RAG.

## Información Clave

### Background
- Former Director of AI at Tesla
- Co-founder of OpenAI
- Known for deep learning education and research
- Active in AI/ML community

### Contribuciones al LLM Wiki
- Created the original "LLM Wiki" idea file
- Advocates for wiki as persistent, compiling artifact
- Pattern: LLM maintains wiki, human curates sources
- Key insight: wiki compounds knowledge over time

### Filosofía
- **Concepts > Code**: Entender fundamentos antes de código
- **AI como herramienta**: Humans dirigen, AI ejecuta
- **Foundations sólidos**: Design patterns, architecture, bundlers antes de frameworks
