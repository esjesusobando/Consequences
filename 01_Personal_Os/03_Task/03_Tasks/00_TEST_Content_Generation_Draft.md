## Draft Generado

**Why your second brain needs a second brain.**

I spent months building the "perfect" productivity system. Notion, Obsidian, Todoist, Linear — you name it, I tried it.

Then I realized the problem wasn't the tools.

It was context.

Every time I switched apps, I lost momentum. Every time I asked an AI assistant to help, it didn't know my goals, my projects, my constraints.

So I built something different.

A PersonalOS that feeds AI the right context, at the right time. Backlog → Tasks → Goals → Daily Standup. Fireflies for meeting context. Engram for memory. QMD for knowledge search.

The result? My AI assistant actually understands what I'm working on.

Not perfect. Still iterating. But 10x more useful than before.

What's your approach to AI-assisted productivity?
