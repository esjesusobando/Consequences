## What You'll Learn

### 1️⃣ How AI Actually Works

- **LLM** = Large Language Model = "autocomplete with a PhD"
- **Tokens** = small pieces of text (~3/4 of a word)
- Has billions of "knobs" (like a 1970s stereo)
- Predicts the next word, one at a time, until it forms complete articles

**Key insight**: AI doesn't "think" — it predicts probabilities. It's like that friend who's so confident they don't know they're wrong.

### 2️⃣ The PART Framework

Everything you want AI to know must go into the **context window**. The trick is putting the right stuff in:

| Letter                          | Means                            | Example                                         |
|--------------------------------|---------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| **P**                           | Prompt                           | What you type                                   |
| **A**                           | Archive                          | Chat history                                    |
| **R**                           | Resources                        | PDFs, images, data                              |
| **T**                           | Tools                            | Browser, APIs, databases                        |

**Metaphor**: The context window is like the notes you leave your future self in "Memento".

### 3️⃣ The 60-30-10 Rule

Don't spend 100% on the same thing. Mix it up:

- **60%** → Repeat what works (iteration)
- **30%** → Improve your prompts
- **10%** → Try new things (experimentation)

### 4️⃣ Meta Prompting

Have a prompt that works? Give it to AI and ask: "Make this prompt better by asking me whatever questions you need."

Invest 10 minutes once → better results forever.

### 5️⃣ Team Strategy

To bring AI to your team:

1. **TRIAGE** → Prioritize most frequent, highest-impact use cases
2. **EXPERIMENT** → Try with prompts and context
3. **AUTOMATE** → When it works, build an agent
4. **MEASURE** → Track results

### 6️⃣ The Future

> "Use AI to test/clarify/elevate your thinking, don't use it to replace your thinking."

Because AI has no emotions, hasn't lived. You have. And that's your biggest edge.
