## 🎯 Key Insights (5 Big Takeaways)

### 1. ChatGPT Wins "Default AI" Race — Lock-in Starting
- **ChatGPT**: 900M weekly users, 2.7x larger than Gemini on web, 8x larger than Claude in subscriptions
- **Context compounds**: más LLM sabe de ti → mejores resultados → más lo usás
- **App stores**: ChatGPT tiene 220 apps, Claude ~160. Flywheel similar a iOS/Android wars
- **ChatGPT going super-app**: Travel, Shopping, Food, Health — consumer transaction categories
- **Claude → prosumer focus**: financial terminals, dev infra, science tools

**→ APLICADO A OIM**: ChatGPT style "default" positioning para OIM — ser el punto de partida para setup/instalación en Atlanta

### 2. Geographic Splintering — 3 Distinct Ecosystems
- **West**: US, India, Brazil, UK, Indonesia (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity)
- **China**: DeepSeek (33.5% traffic), Doubao, Kimi (requiere data onshore, censorship rules)
- **Russia**: DeepSeek (7.1%), Yandex Browser+Alice (71M MAU), GigaChat
- **Per capita adoption**: Singapore #1, UAE #2, Hong Kong #3, South Korea #4. US ranks #20

**→ APLICADO**: OIM targeting Atlanta primarily → US market. No geographic diversification needed yet

### 3. Creative Tools Evolution
- **Image generation**: declining (Midjourney fell from top 10 to #46)
- **Video**: Kling AI, Hailuo, Pixverse rising. Veo 3 closed gap. Chinese models lead
- **Music/Voice**: Suno (#15), ElevenLabs (every edition since 2023) — defensible niches
- **Sora**: viral first 20 days, now 3M daily active users as video creation tool

**→ APLICADO A Marian Diamond**: "Creative" en el branding de la landing no es diferenciador. Focus en Trust/Professional/Local

### 4. Agents Are Here (Vibe Coding → Horizontal Agents)
- **Vibe coding still strong**: Replit, Lovable, Cursor, Claude Code — revenue growing
- **Horizontal agents**: OpenClaw (68k GitHub stars in weeks, acquired by OpenAI Feb 2026)
- **Manus**: acquired by Meta for ~$2B. Genspark: $300M Series B, $100M ARR
- **Pattern**: donde el modelo gigante NO ha focuseado, hay espacio para products

**→ LEARNED**: Don't build where Google/OpenAI are focused. Find the whitespace

### 5. AI Beyond Browser/App — OS Layer Emerging
- **Browser as AI product**: Atlas (OpenAI), Comet (Perplexity), Dia (Browser Company → Atlassian)
- **Desktop-native**: Claude Code ($1B ARR in 6 months!), OpenAI Codex (2M weekly users, 25% weekly growth)
- **Embedded AI**: Claude in Excel/PowerPoint, ChatGPT for Excel, Gemini in Workspace
- **Methodology undercounting**: people who use AI heavily (8hrs/day in Claude Code) don't register in web traffic

**→ APLICADO**: AI is moving from destination to FEATURE. Future projects should think "AI as layer" not "AI as product"
